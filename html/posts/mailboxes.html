Appreciating the cute mailboxes in my neighbourhood

March 13, 2026

I live in a semi-rural neighbourhood outside of the main city and one of the things I like most is how different some of the mailboxes are from each other. About half of them are basic ones bought from a store but the other half seem to be custom made where each has its own charm. This post is simply to share some of the mailboxes that make me happy every time I see them.

The interesting thing about all these fun mailboxes is that they haven't served a purpose for quite some time. A long time ago, my areas public postal service installed community mailboxes and delivers the neighbourhood's mail there. Amazon delivers packages get delivered right to your door. My parents house (where I live) doesn't even have a mailbox! Maybe these mailboxes will start to disappear or maybe people will keep them for years to come just because. I hope they stick around.
